Privacy Policy — قلم (Qalam)

Last updated: 8 October 2026

خلاصہ: قلم آپ کے اپنے کمپیوٹر پر چلتا ہے۔ نہ کوئی اکاؤنٹ، نہ ٹیلی میٹری، نہ اشتہار۔ آپ کی تحریر یا آواز صرف اُس وقت آلے سے باہر جاتی ہے جب آپ خود کوئی اختیاری AI سہولت چلائیں — پروف ریڈنگ، شعر کی تشریح، لفظ کے معنی، املا (ڈکٹیشن) یا بلند خوانی — اور وہ بھی آپ ہی کی API کلید سے۔

Qalam is a word processor that runs on your own computer. It is built so that your writing stays yours.

What Qalam collects

Nothing. Qalam has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The developer receives no information about you or your documents.

Where your documents live

Your documents are stored as files on your own device, in the location you choose. Qalam does not upload, back up, or synchronise them anywhere. Crash-recovery snapshots are likewise stored only on your device.

Qalam also stores small preferences on your device — your interface language, zoom level, default font and size, your personal spelling dictionary, your تخلص (pen name) for شعر cards, and any AI API key you choose to enter. These never leave your device except as described below.

When text or audio leaves your device: the optional AI features

Qalam includes optional AI features. All of them are off unless you set them up with your own API key, and each runs only when you explicitly invoke it:

  • AI proofreading — sends the text being proofread (the document or your selection) to the service you chose.
  • Couplet explanation — sends the couplet you selected, together with the metre Qalam identified for it, to the service you chose.
  • Word meaning — sends the word you asked about and the sentence it appears in, so the meaning can be read in context.
  • Urdu dictation — records audio only while you are dictating and sends that recording for transcription.
  • Read aloud — sends the passage being read (from the cursor, or your selection), one paragraph at a time, to be turned into speech.

Proofreading, couplet explanation and word meaning use Google Gemini (see Google's privacy terms for the Gemini API) or Anthropic Claude (see Anthropic's privacy policy), whichever you chose; dictation uses Google Gemini; read aloud uses Microsoft Azure AI Speech (see Microsoft's privacy statement), in the Azure region you chose.

Each request travels over an encrypted connection using your own API key, so your relationship for that request is directly with that provider, under their terms. Qalam does not proxy, copy, log, or retain the text or audio; the speech it plays is kept only in temporary files on your device, deleted when reading stops. Nothing is sent in the background, on a schedule, or without your action.

Other network connections

  • Update check (direct downloads only) — the version of Qalam distributed outside the App Store checks the public releases page once a day to see whether a newer version exists. The request contains no personal information or document content. The App Store version makes no such request; updates come through the App Store itself.
  • App Store purchases — the optional Qalam Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.

If you never use the AI features, Qalam makes no other network connections.

Spellcheck and poetry tools

Spellchecking, poetry alignment, and تقطیع (metrical scansion) are entirely offline. The dictionary is bundled with the app, and words you add to your personal dictionary stay on your device.

Children

Qalam is a general-purpose writing tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
